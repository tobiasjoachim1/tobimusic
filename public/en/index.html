Turning Your Ideas into Release-Ready Songs

Pre-Production

We begin by clarifying your vision and setting clear goals for the project. Together, we define the sound, vibe, and direction of your song so that every step that follows feels intentional.

Songwriting

Whether you’ve already written a full song or just have a few ideas, I can help bring everything together. I support you with refining melodies, lyrics, and chord progressions, as well as finding the right instrumentation, tempo, and rhythm — making sure each element fits your vision and feels natural.

Production & Recording

This is where your song takes shape. I produce the instrumental based on your vision and share drafts along the way to ensure we’re heading in the right direction. Once the instrumental is ready, you can record your vocals — either on your own, or with my guidance in person or remotely. I’ll then edit and polish the vocals so everything sounds cohesive and professional.

Mixing & Mastering

This is the final step, where your song comes together. Mixing and mastering bring balance, clarity, and energy to the track, making it sound polished and ready for release. I make sure every element works in harmony and highlights the heart of your song.

Pick What You Need

You can work with me through the entire process from start to finish or choose just the parts you need. Either way, I’ll make sure your song feels right to you.
